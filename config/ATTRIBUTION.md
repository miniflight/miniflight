# Asset attribution

`data/whoop.glb` is an original display model generated by `data/whoop.py`.
Its 75 mm layout, four circular guards, small camera and bottom propellers
use the [FlyLens 75](https://flywoo.net/products/flylens-75-frame-kit) as a
visual reference. Regenerate it with `python3.11 config/data/whoop.py`.
The dimensions and shapes are approximate.

The local Three.js runtime and `GLTFLoader` come from
[Three.js 0.165.0](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r165) under the MIT License.
